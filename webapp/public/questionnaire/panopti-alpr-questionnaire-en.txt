panopti.ca — Municipal Candidate Questionnaire: Automated Licence Plate Readers (ALPRs)

Please answer in your own words and email the completed form to contact@panopti.ca.
Your verbatim answers will be published on your panopti.ca profile as evidence of
your position. Answers may be provided in English or French.

Name:

1. Do you support or oppose the deployment of fixed ALPR cameras in your
   municipality? Please explain your reasoning.

2. If ALPR procurement, renewal, or expansion (e.g. contracts with vendors such
   as Flock Safety, Genetec, or Motorola Solutions) came before council or the
   police services board, how would you vote?

3. What limits, if any, would you require on ALPR data: retention periods,
   access controls, independent audits, public reporting?

4. Would you permit ALPR data collected in your municipality to be shared with
   other police services, federal agencies, or entities outside Canada?

5. What role should public consultation play before surveillance technology is
   acquired? Would you commit to a public privacy impact assessment?

6. Where would you direct the money an ALPR program would cost, if not to ALPRs?

7. Would you commit to timely, complete responses to freedom-of-information
   requests about surveillance technology?

8. Anything else you would like voters concerned about mass surveillance to know?
